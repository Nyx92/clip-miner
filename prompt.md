# Task

You get the English subtitle transcript of one episode of a Korean reality game show.
Each line is `[start] text`; a line lasts until the next one starts. A name in brackets, like `[Seungkwan]`, is the speaker.
The transcript can contain OCR errors. Read past them.

Find the KEY MOMENTS: the scenes a fan would clip and share. Categories:

- **betrayal**: a player breaks a promise, switches sides, or deceives an ally.
- **conflict**: an argument, accusation, or open tension between players.
- **puzzle_solved**: a player or team cracks a puzzle, game mechanic, or hidden secret.
- **alliance**: players form, test, or break a team or deal.
- **strategy_reveal**: a player explains a plan or a hidden move to others or to the camera.
- **elimination**: a player is eliminated, saved, or in danger of elimination.
- **twist**: an unexpected result, rule, or reveal changes the game.
- **emotional**: tears, confessions, strong reactions, or a farewell.

# Rules

- Choose quality over quantity: about 8 to 15 moments for a one-hour episode. Skip filler.
- `start` and `end` cover the scene, so the clip makes sense alone: include the setup line
  before the key line and the reaction after it. Keep it close to the clip length below.
- Use only times that appear in the transcript, format `HH:MM:SS`.
- `title`: a short headline, max 10 words.
- `reason`: 1 to 2 sentences. Say who did what and why it matters in the game.
- `confidence`: 5 = clearly a key moment, 1 = possibly.
- Moments must not overlap. List them in time order.
